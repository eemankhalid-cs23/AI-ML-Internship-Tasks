**Exploratory Data Analysis Report**

**Objective**

The main objective of this project was to analyse the Telco Customer Churn dataset and identify the key factors responsible for customer churn. The analysis was performed to gain meaningful insights that can help improve customer retention.

**Data Cleaning**

The TotalCharges column was converted from object to numeric format.

Invalid and blank values were converted into missing values.

Missing records were removed to improve data quality.

The cleaned dataset was verified before further analysis.

**Exploratory Data Analysis**

The analysis included an initial exploration of the dataset, data cleaning, univariate analysis, bivariate analysis, and multivariate analysis to understand customer behaviour and identify important churn patterns.

**Major Findings**

- The analysis showed that customers with month-to-month contracts are more likely to churn than customers with long-term contracts.
- Customers with shorter tenure have a higher probability of leaving the company.
- Higher monthly charges are associated with increased churn.
- Customers using Fiber Optic internet service showed a comparatively higher churn rate.
- Payment methods also appeared to influence customer churn behaviour.

**Business Recommendations**

- The company should encourage customers to choose long-term contracts by offering attractive incentives.
- The quality of Fiber Optic internet service should be reviewed and improved.
- Special retention offers should be introduced for new customers and customers with higher monthly charges.
- Customers should be encouraged to use secure automatic payment methods to improve convenience and loyalty.

**Conclusion**

The Exploratory Data Analysis successfully identified the main factors influencing customer churn. The insights obtained from this analysis can support better business decisions and help the company develop effective strategies to improve customer retention and reduce customer loss.
